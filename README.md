# Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of retail sales, customer behavior, product performance, geographical trends, and product returns. The goal of the project is to transform raw retail data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

The dashboard is designed for business stakeholders, managers, and analysts to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify growth opportunities, understand customer purchasing patterns, and improve overall business performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Identify top-performing and underperforming products.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and segmentation.
- Evaluate regional sales performance across different locations.
- Track product returns and their impact on profitability.
- Enable detailed product and customer-level analysis through drill-through reports.

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Dashboard
Provides a high-level overview of business performance.

**Key Insights:**
- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Revenue Trends
- Business KPIs
- Performance Summary

---

### 2. Product Analytics

Analyze product performance and profitability.

**Features:**
- Top Selling Products
- Product Category Analysis
- Revenue by Product
- Product Performance Comparison
- Product Contribution to Sales

---

### 3. Customer Analytics

Understand customer behavior and engagement.

**Features:**
- Customer Segmentation
- High-Value Customers
- Purchase Trends
- Customer Distribution
- Customer Performance Metrics

---

### 4. Geography Analytics

Evaluate regional sales performance.

**Features:**
- Sales by Region
- Geographic Performance Analysis
- Regional Revenue Distribution
- Location-Based Insights

---

### 5. Returns Analysis

Monitor product return patterns and business impact.

**Features:**
- Return Rate Analysis
- Returned Product Tracking
- Return Trends
- Impact on Revenue

---

### 6. Product Detail Drill-Through

Provides detailed insights for individual products.

**Features:**
- Product-Level Metrics
- Sales Trends
- Product Performance Details

---

### 7. Customer Detail Drill-Through

Provides detailed customer-level insights.

**Features:**
- Customer Purchase History
- Customer Value Analysis
- Behavioral Insights

---

### 8. Custom Tooltips

Enhanced user experience through interactive tooltip pages that provide additional context and detailed information.
